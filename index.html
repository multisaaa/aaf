Raw-material confidence

A hardboard manufacturer supported by an established wood-supply network.

AAF is a member of SHAIYO TRIPLE A GROUP, a Thai business group with established experience in woodchip production and export across Southeast Asia. This relationship strengthens access to plantation-grown eucalyptus and supports continuity and consistency in raw-material supply.

Our factory in Chok Chai, Nakhon Ratchasima, is close to key eucalyptus-growing areas and has convenient access to Thailand's major seaports. AAF supplies raw hardboard sheets, rectangular cut-to-size panels and profile-cut components made to customer drawings.

Plantation-grown eucalyptus fibres are formed in water and consolidated under heat and pressure. The wood fibre's natural lignin provides the primary bonding mechanism.
