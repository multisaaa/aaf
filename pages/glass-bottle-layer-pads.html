Common buying questions

Information to confirm before quotation.

What information is needed for a layer-pad quotation?

Provide the pallet pattern, finished dimensions, thickness, corner detail, quantity, annual consumption, destination and any customer drawing or acceptance criteria.

Does AAF design the complete bottle pallet?

AAF supplies raw hardboard sheets or cut hardboard components. The customer validates the complete pallet configuration under its actual bottle, handling and line conditions.

Can AAF supply samples for a line trial?

Sample and line-trial requirements can be reviewed after the dimensions, grade, quantity and acceptance criteria are confirmed.
